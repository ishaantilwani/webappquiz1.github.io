Robotics Club!

Description:

Robotics club lets you dive into the world of technology, participating in Competitions like FGC and FRC and FLL.

You can gain a lot of experience and knowledge from these challenges.

The club supervisor is Mr. Serginho Pech.

You can do activities such as 3D modelling, building FGC robots using the FGC Kit and coding the robots. The club does require Safety glasses.

The club is Open to anyone ready to collaborate on building and coding with no prior experience needed.

Club Advisor: Mr. Serginho Pech
