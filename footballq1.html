Football Club!

Description:

The football club is a welcoming and perfect space to learn football, and play friendly matches.

The goal is to practice the 5 fundamentals of football, Pace, Shooting, Dribbling, Passing, Defending, Physicality.

There will be daily training drills and short matches to boost experience and confidence.

Requires athletic shoes, a water bottle, and a readiness to run and work as a team, and must be or above the age of 13.
